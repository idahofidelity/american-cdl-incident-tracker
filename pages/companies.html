Incident rates by carrier. Both raw counts and per-driver rates shown separately for fair comparison.
How per-driver rates are calculated: Driver counts sourced from FMCSA Motor Carrier Census (self-reported by carriers, updated daily). When CDL driver count is available, it is used. When not, total drivers is used. When neither is available, registered power units serve as a proxy denominator. Raw counts and rates are displayed separately — a large carrier with many incidents may have a lower rate than a small carrier. Both views matter. FMCSA safety ratings shown where available.
